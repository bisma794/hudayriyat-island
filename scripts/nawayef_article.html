    Nawayef East Hills by Modon

    Nawayef East Hills is an exclusive enclave of ultra-luxury villas and mansions nestled on the elevated terrain of Hudayriyat Island, Abu Dhabi. Masterfully developed by Modon, this prestigious neighborhood offers sweeping views of the coastline, lush greenery, and the Abu Dhabi skyline. Designed for those who value privacy, space, and refinement, Nawayef East Hills features expansive 4 to 8-bedroom residences that blend modern architectural elegance with functional living. With world-class amenities, multi-level floor plans including basements, and high-end finishes throughout, Nawayef East Hills redefines island luxury living in the UAE. Set for handover in 2026, this freehold community open to all nationalities.
