Property Details

Bashayer Villas comprises two distinguished villa collections — Select Villas (4-bedroom) and Shore Villas (5-bedroom) — each conceived for modern family living through generous layouts and premium craftsmanship. The 4-bedroom Select Villas span a total GSA of 365.99 sqm, featuring dual kitchens, expansive family living areas, dedicated dining spaces, staff quarters, and sweeping outdoor terraces. The 5-bedroom Shore Villas elevate the experience further with grand master suites, multiple living areas, and heightened privacy throughout.Every residence is thoughtfully engineered to optimize natural light, ventilation, and spatial flow, forging a seamless dialogue between indoor comfort and outdoor serenity for a truly elevated lifestyle.

 

Lifestyle & Amenities

Life at Bashayer Villas is defined by a premium coastal rhythm, supported by an impressive suite of modern amenities. At its heart, a vibrant community centre offers retail outlets, dining destinations, and essential conveniences, complemented by lush parks, scenic walking trails, and open landscaped spaces.

Crowning the experience, the rooftop infinity pool delivers sweeping waterfront panoramas, transforming everyday living into a resort-style escape.

Location & Connectivity

Perfectly positioned on Hudayriyat Island, Bashayer Villas grants direct access to Abu Dhabi's premier leisure and lifestyle destinations, including the Hudayriyat Leisure District, Marsana Beach, and scenic cycling parks close to home.The development ensures effortless connectivity to key destinations such as Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Saadiyat Island, Yas Island, and Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM), all within a convenient 15 to 35-minute drive.
