Bashayer Villas – Premium Waterfront Villas on Hudayriyat Island, Abu Dhabi

Bashayer Villas redefines waterfront luxury in Abu Dhabi, marrying contemporary design sensibilities with the calm of a coastal setting. Nestled within one of the city's most vibrant lifestyle destinations, this exclusive villa enclave is purpose-built for families in search of privacy, space, and an enduring connection to nature. Every detail has been considered with precision and elegance — expansive villas, lush green surroundings, and world-class amenities converge to deliver a sophisticated, future-ready way of life.

 

Key Project Highlights

  • Exclusive 4 and 5-bedroom villas with expansive layouts and private outdoor sanctuaries
  • Coveted waterfront address on Hudayriyat Island framed by landscaped surroundings
  • Signature clubhouse featuring a rooftop infinity pool and curated lifestyle facilities
  • Secure gated enclave designed for privacy and family-centric living
  • Contemporary architecture drawing inspiration from coastal textures and natural elements
  • Effortless connectivity to Abu Dhabi's most celebrated landmarks and leisure destinations
  • Investor-friendly 50/50 payment plan with handover in March 2029

 

Property Details

Bashayer Villas comprises two distinguished villa collections — Select Villas (4-bedroom) and Shore Villas (5-bedroom) — each conceived for modern family living through generous layouts and premium craftsmanship. The 4-bedroom Select Villas span a total GSA of 365.99 sqm, featuring dual kitchens, expansive family living areas, dedicated dining spaces, staff quarters, and sweeping outdoor terraces. The 5-bedroom Shore Villas elevate the experience further with grand master suites, multiple living areas, and heightened privacy throughout.Every residence is thoughtfully engineered to optimize natural light, ventilation, and spatial flow, forging a seamless dialogue between indoor comfort and outdoor serenity for a truly elevated lifestyle.

 

Lifestyle & Amenities

Life at Bashayer Villas is defined by a premium coastal rhythm, supported by an impressive suite of modern amenities. At its heart, a vibrant community centre offers retail outlets, dining destinations, and essential conveniences, complemented by lush parks, scenic walking trails, and open landscaped spaces.
Wellness-focused residents can turn to dedicated sports courts, jogging tracks, cycling paths, and a state-of-the-art gymnasium, while families enjoy secure children's play areas and inviting community gathering spaces. Crowning the experience, the rooftop infinity pool delivers sweeping waterfront panoramas, transforming everyday living into a resort-style escape.

 

Architecture & Interior

Bashayer Villas embodies contemporary coastal architecture — clean lines, neutral palettes, and natural materials come together in seamless harmony with the surrounding landscape. Interiors are composed with open-plan layouts, soaring ceilings, and premium finishes that lend an air of modern elegance to every room.Floor-to-ceiling windows invite natural light throughout while framing stunning views of the waterfront and landscaped grounds. Thoughtful functional elements — dual kitchens, staff rooms, and generously proportioned living areas — make these villas the perfect setting for comfortable family living infused with luxury.

 

Location & Connectivity

Perfectly positioned on Hudayriyat Island, Bashayer Villas grants direct access to Abu Dhabi's premier leisure and lifestyle destinations, including the Hudayriyat Leisure District, Marsana Beach, and scenic cycling parks close to home.The development ensures effortless connectivity to key destinations such as Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Saadiyat Island, Yas Island, and Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM), all within a convenient 15 to 35-minute drive. Downtown Dubai lies just around 90 minutes away, offering the ideal balance of convenience and tranquility for professionals and families alike.

 

Bashayer Villas is far more than a residential address — it is a luxury lifestyle destination defined by exclusivity, comfort, and enduring investment value in Abu Dhabi's most sought-after waterfront setting.
